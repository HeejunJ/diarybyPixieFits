One page a day —
what you wore, and how it felt.

A shopping app knows what you bought. It has no idea what you actually wear. Diary by PixieFits is the other half of that: the record of what you reached for, what worked, and what you would change.

Companion to PixieFits · One account, one closet

A diary that knows your wardrobe

Every page is a day: the outfit, how it felt, the weather it had to survive, and a few lines in your own words.

Build the outfit

Pick a whole look, or lay it out piece by piece on a board — drag to move, two fingers to size and turn. It is saved as a layout, not a flat picture, so it stays editable months later.

A month at a glance

The calendar shows the outfit you wore on each day, so a month of the diary reads as a contact sheet rather than a grid of numbers.

What you actually wear

Marking a look as worn builds a record over time. After a month it can tell you what you reach for — which is not usually what you bought.

The weather, already filled in

Choose your city once and each page fills in its own weather. No location permission, no GPS — you type a city name, and that is all that leaves the phone.

One closet, two apps

Diary by PixieFits and PixieFits share an account and a closet. A look you save in PixieFits appears here. A garment you cut out here appears there. They are not two copies — they are the same records.

Cut out on your phone

Photograph a garment laid flat and the background comes off on the device. The photo is not uploaded to do it, and no image service ever sees it.

Questions

Do I need a PixieFits account?

You can write pages without one — they stay on the device. Signing in with your PixieFits account is what brings your closet across and backs the diary up.

Is anything shared or public?

No. Your pages are readable only by you. There is no feed, no followers and no sharing in the app.

Can I delete everything?

Yes — Me → Delete your account removes your pages, your wear history and the pieces you cut out, then closes the account. See the privacy policy.
